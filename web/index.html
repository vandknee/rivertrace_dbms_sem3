Pollution Analysis

Review stations with reported ranges at or beyond the selected limits.

A review list, not a source verdict. Stations are ranked by severity. Upstream flags suggest places to investigate; connectivity does not prove the cause of pollution.
How is severity calculated?

The project adds normalised exceedances for maximum BOD, minimum DO, pH range and maximum faecal coliform. This is a simple project score, not an official water quality index. It does not combine simultaneous samples.

BOD: max(0, BODmax / 3 − 1); DO: max(0, (5 − DOmin) / 5); pH: max(0, 6.5 − pHmin) + max(0, pHmax − 8.5); FC: max(0, FCmax / 2500 − 1). Missing parameters are skipped; exact boundary values are flagged even if their score contribution is zero.
